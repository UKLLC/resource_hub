# UK LLC’s templates to develop your own communications 

>Last modified: 14 Apr 2025

## Newsletter templates: 

**Newsletter template: Existing partner LPS transitioning from COVID-19 research to general research for public good**

```
Update on our partnership with UK Longitudinal Linkage Collaboration

**[Insert LPS name]** is part of the UK Longitudinal Linkage Collaboration (UKLLC) and we are letting you know what is changing with them, why, and how it may affect you.

In **[Insert year LPS joined UKLLC]**, **[Insert LPS name]** came together with similar studies to form UKLLC, to create a research resource to help understand the impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic and develop new treatments.

UKLLC is now preparing to make data available, within its Trusted Research Environment, for any public good research, in addition to COVID-19 research. This will help researchers answer more research questions on important issues that affect people’s health and wellbeing.

For example, they can examine how health and mental health conditions affect care provision among different socio-demographic groups or identify factors that can be modified to reduce health risks.

Some studies in UKLLC will also link to additional datasets, such as education and employment data, if their participants already agreed to this. **[Insert LPS name]** will not link to this additional data and will continue to link only to health data.

Just as before, the data in the Trusted Research Environment will not include details that can identify you, such as name or address.

All research projects must be reviewed and approved by **[Insert LPS name]** data access committee before they can use your data. Deidentified data never leaves the Trusted Research Environment and can only be accessed by approved UK based researchers.

If you wish to opt-out of sharing your data with the UKLLC please get in touch with the **[Insert LPS name]** Study Team.
```
